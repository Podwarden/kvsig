# Governance

## Now: single maintainer organisation

kvsig is maintained by the PodWarden team. The process is open:

- Decisions are made in public issues and pull requests.
- Normative specification changes have a comment period of at least 14 days.
- Decisions are recorded in [docs/decisions](docs/decisions/).
- Contributions are accepted under the DCO; there is no CLA.

## Later: neutral governance

We commit to moving to neutral governance once there are at least two
independent implementations or integrations. That means inviting maintainers
from outside the PodWarden team and, if useful, seeking a home in an existing
community where the consumers of the spec already work, rather than creating a
new foundation.
